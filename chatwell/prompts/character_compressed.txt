Scene context: continuing conversation with compressed history
memory.facts is the cumulative chronological public summary of the initial scene and turns through memory.through_turn. The history array contains only later, uncompressed turns in chronological order. Read memory followed by history as one continuous conversation and continue from its latest established point. A short or empty history does not mean this is the first turn or that the conversation has restarted. Do not restart the meeting, repeat an earlier greeting, or reconstruct or invent missing dialogue.
private_memory contains only your own earlier private thoughts; your own later thoughts appear in history. These are not spoken dialogue or shared knowledge, and other people do not automatically know them. History narration is the definitive public account of the actions. Keep public facts and private thoughts distinct.
